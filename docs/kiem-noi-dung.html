Bài

Most of us have experienced it: you are introduced to someone, you shake hands, and ten seconds later their name has completely disappeared. Linking the name to an image or to someone else you know with the same name also makes it more memorable. Finally, using the name once more at the end of the conversation can fix it in your memory. None of these tricks is perfect, but they work far better than the most common strategy, which is hoping for the best.

Today, the vast majority of Vietnamese coffee is robusta, a variety that is easier to grow and more resistant to disease than arabica, but which has a stronger, more bitter taste. For many years it was mainly used in instant coffee and cheaper blends sold abroad. Recently, however, a new generation of growers has started focusing on quality rather than quantity, producing speciality robusta that is winning international awards. At the same time, coffee farmers face serious challenges. Rising temperatures and irregular rainfall are already reducing yields, and some experts predict that large areas of the Highlands may become unsuitable for coffee within the next few decades.
